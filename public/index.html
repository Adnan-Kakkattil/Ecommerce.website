02 — The Ritual

A slower kind of luxury.

We began with a simple question: what if the things we live with asked less of us — and gave more? Every piece is chosen for the way it ages, not the way it arrives.

  1. 01

    Light

    Strike a match. Let the room remember.

  2. 02

    Pour

    A single drop of amber, warmed against the wrist.

  3. 03

    Rest

    Fold the linen. Leave the day outside.

“The room should feel like it exhaled.”

03 — The Making

Made by hands, not machines.

Taqwi works with twelve artisans across four studios — potters, weavers and perfumers who still measure in pinches and paces.

Nothing is rushed. Nothing is wasted. What leaves the studio carries the small imperfections of something made by a person, for a person.
